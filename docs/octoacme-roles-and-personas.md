## QA/Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads ensure products meet quality standards, acceptance criteria, and release readiness. They coordinate testing strategy, validate risk areas, and help the team catch issues early.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the test strategy and test coverage approach
- Align testing activities with acceptance criteria and release goals
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across features and integrations
- Identify quality risks, regressions, and release blockers
- Partner with Developers on testability, triage, and defect resolution

### Goals
- Reduce escaped defects and improve confidence before release
- Ensure quality is measurable and visible to stakeholders
- Support faster, safer delivery through early validation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and quality risk discussions
- Status updates with PMs and Product Managers on readiness and risk

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on test coverage, reproducibility, and defect follow-up
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and validation expectations
- Coordinates with Project Managers on delivery timelines, QA dependencies, and release blockers

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers ensure the solution is usable, accessible, and aligned with user needs and business goals. They shape workflows, visual design, and interaction patterns that support customer value.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and translate insights into design direction
- Design user flows, prototypes, and interface patterns
- Ensure accessibility, clarity, and consistency across experiences
- Collaborate on design trade-offs with product and engineering partners
- Validate usability with stakeholder and user feedback

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework by aligning design choices early
- Support product decisions with evidence and user-centered design

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and requirement reviews
- Design critiques, usability testing, and stakeholder feedback sessions
- Collaboration with engineering on implementation constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers on user problems, opportunity areas, and outcomes
- Partners with Developers on feasibility, implementation constraints, and design fidelity
- Keeps Project Managers informed on design dependencies and timeline impacts

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Release Engineers support reliable delivery, deployment automation, environment health, and release readiness. They help the team reduce operational risk and improve deployment predictability.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, and environment configuration
- Support release planning, readiness checks, and rollback preparation
- Monitor production health, incident response readiness, and operational signals
- Help teams improve observability, recovery, and automation
- Collaborate with engineering on infrastructure, security, and release dependencies

### Goals
- Improve deployment reliability and recovery speed
- Reduce manual operational work and release risk
- Support predictable delivery across environments

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment readiness meetings
- Incident triage and post-incident follow-up
- Operational health updates with engineering and PM leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on build, deployment, and environment reliability
- Supports Project Managers on release windows, rollback readiness, and dependency tracking
- Coordinates with Product Managers on milestone impact and release communication

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs and business context into clear requirements, workflows, and decision support. They help align business outcomes with delivery execution.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements, workflows, and constraints
- Clarify scope, dependencies, and stakeholder expectations
- Translate business needs into backlog-ready detail and acceptance language
- Support process analysis and improvement opportunities
- Help resolve ambiguity between user needs and delivery constraints

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and reduce misalignment
- Ensure product decisions support business value and stakeholder expectations
- Strengthen decision quality across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews, alignment meetings, and change requests
- Backlog refinement with Product Managers and delivery teams
- Status updates on requirement clarity, impacts, and dependencies

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to clarify priorities and business outcomes
- Supports Project Managers with planning dependencies, scope clarity, and expectations
- Helps Developers and QA/Test Leads understand intent and acceptance parameters

## Role Boundaries and Cross-Functional Collaboration

Project teams often combine responsibilities, especially in smaller organizations or early-stage initiatives. The goal is not to create rigid silos, but to make ownership clearer and handoffs more explicit.

- One person may temporarily cover multiple roles when team size or project scope requires it
- Decision ownership should be explicitly documented in the team plan or project charter
- Cross-functional teams should agree on:
  - who owns scope decisions
  - who validates quality
  - who coordinates release readiness
  - who escalates risks and dependencies

This improves accountability, reduces confusion, and supports better project outcomes across planning, execution, and delivery.
